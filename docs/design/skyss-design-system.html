01 — Principles

What this system is

Honest

Structure is visible. Borders are real and heavy. No blurred shadows pretending to float. Elements sit on the surface and own their edges. Everything is what it looks like.

Warm contrast

The tension between amber warmth and navy cold is the system's core energy — directly from the Skyss illustration. That dynamic drives every color decision.

Typographic weight

Syne's heavy display weight carries hierarchy. DM Mono grounds technical and labeling contexts. Instrument Serif adds editorial warmth as a counterpoint.

Restrained motion

Transitions are short and functional — translate + box-shadow on hover, nothing else. Motion communicates state, not delight.
